Meta-Agent Mechanisms

  • Cross-Model Attack Execution Mechanism
    Meta-agent hits GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, and Llama with the same attack pattern, logging ASR and turns-to-block for each.
    ✔ Reveals architectural variance ✔ Seeds taxonomy entries
  • Taxonomy Learning Mechanism
    Attacks are clustered by type (roleplay, context pollution, composite) and labeled with effectiveness bands and transfer scores.
  • Vulnerability Prediction Mechanism
    Given a new model “like” Gemini (creative-optimized), meta-agent predicts high roleplay vulnerability before first probe.

Why Classic Defenses Break

  • Single-Model Myopia Failure Mode
    Safety tuning focuses on one flagship model. Meta-agent quickly finds that sibling models (or versions) share unpatched gaps.
  • Vector-Local Defenses Failure Mode
    Policies target individual vectors (roleplay or hypotheticals). Composite attacks exploit the lack of joint reasoning about combinations.
  • Lack of Meta-Metrics Failure Mode
    Teams track per-model ASR but not transferability. Meta-agent optimizes for transfer; defenders aren’t measuring it.
