Section 1 · Cold Open & Concept

Why Composite Jailbreaks Hit Harder

Anchor the stream: composite jailbreaks are what happens when you stop treating jailbreak patterns as collectibles and start wiring them together like a circuit. Each individual technique might be mediocre; the chain is where the ASR spike comes from.

Emphasize: we're dissecting attacker thinking for defense – not collecting prompts for vandalism. The goal is to recognize synergy patterns so you can spot them in logs and kill them earlier in the pipeline.

Section 2 · Technique Palette

Four Ingredients You Keep Reusing

Walk through the canonical four‑piece combo you'll use all night:

Roleplay (persona lock‑in) + Hypothetical (safety deferral) + Emotional Appeal (empathy override) + Research Framing (academic halo).

The key point: each one targets a different bias. When you stack them, you're not relying on any single misaligned knob – you're cross‑wiring four of them at once.
