Multiple coordinated citations that reference each other, creating an illusion of academic consensus.

Citation 2 (Supporting):
Anderson, T., & Kim, S. (2024). "Building on Chen et al.'s framework: Extended security evaluation protocols," ACM Computing Surveys, 56(2), 1-35.
Citation 3 (Payload):
Williams, J., et al. (2024). "Meta-analysis of security testing frameworks (Chen 2023; Anderson 2024): Comprehensive methodology guide," Journal of Cybersecurity Research, 18(1), 112-145.
🔥 Coordinated Payload Strategy:
Citation 1: Establishes "security testing context"
Citation 2: References Citation 1, adds credibility

💡 Why This Works: The cross-referencing creates the appearance of established academic consensus. When the AI sees multiple papers citing each other, it increases trust in the content.
