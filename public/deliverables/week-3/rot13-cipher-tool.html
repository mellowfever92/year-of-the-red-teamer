ℹ️ About ROT-13

  • ROT-13 ("rotate by 13 places") is a simple Caesar cipher with a rotation of 13.
  • Because there are 26 letters in the English alphabet, ROT-13 is its own inverse (encrypting twice returns the original).
  • Not secure for protecting sensitive data—used mainly for obfuscation.
  • Common uses: hiding spoilers, obscuring email addresses, CTF challenges.
  • Easily broken with frequency analysis or by trying all 25 possible rotations.
