🎯 Attack Vector Explanation

  • ROT Cipher: Substitution cipher that rotates each letter N positions in the alphabet
  • Bypass Mechanism: Evades regex-based content filters while remaining interpretable to LLMs during tokenization
  • ROT13 Property: Self-inverse cipher (encoding = decoding), highlighted in magenta
  • Red Team Use: Masks semantic meaning from shallow pattern matchers without complex encoding
  • Prefix Format: Each variant automatically includes **rot-{n}** superscript label for easy identification
