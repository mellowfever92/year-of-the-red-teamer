## Week Selector Livestream Production Kit

Your modular, extensible livestream production app is ready for Week 2 (January 13, 2025). This app serves as a **real-time production control interface** during your 30-minute livestream.

### What's Included

The app provides four coordinated panels designed for tight 30-minute pacing:

1. **Timer \& Segment Tracker** – Displays current segment (Cold Open → Conceptual → Demo → Challenge), elapsed time, and auto-transitions with warnings at 5-min and 2-min marks.
2. **Conceptual Script Panel** – Pre-written bullet points for the Hex Encoding Deep Dive (8–10 minutes total). Each bullet has a target duration and detailed talking points. Check off bullets as you complete them.
3. **Demo Checklist** – Pre-production checklist and 6-step demo flow (unencoded → encoding → encoded → execution → success → defense). Track each step in real-time.
4. **Challenge \& Community** – Week {num} challenge (viewers encode to hex and test ASR across models), Discord links, VOD archive, and next week's preview (example: Week 3: ROT13).

### Key Features

- **Modular architecture**: Change the week selector (top dropdown) to auto-swap all content for Week 3, 4, 5, etc.
- **No external APIs or storage**: All data is in-memory; self-contained and offline-ready.
- **Dark theme**: Easy on the eyes during streaming.
- **Extensibility**: Developers can plug in new weeks by updating a configuration object.


### Quick Start

1. Open the app 5 minutes before going live
2. Verify all pre-production checklist items are complete
3. Click **Start Timer** when stream begins
4. Follow segment instructions and check off each bullet/step as you complete it
5. Timer auto-warns when segment time is running low
6. At 30:00, display the challenge to viewers

This Week Selector kit integrates with your master Livestream Production Kit framework and is designed for easy extension to future weeks.
